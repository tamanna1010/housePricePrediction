Downsides of Long context:
=========================
- Higher API costs (# Input token)
- Longer completion/inference times
- Content/documents in the middle may be overlooked (lost in the middle)
- Ongoing research to address 'context limitation'.

RAG: 
===
The main problem that is solved with RAG architecture is knowledge gap. This approach enhances the accuracy and relevance of responses.

Main components:
- Index & Embed
- Vector Store
- Retrieval
- Filtering and Re-ranking
- Prompt Augmentation
- Generation

RAG = 'Dense vector Retrieval' + 'In-context learning' with preparation step as 'document embedding'.
